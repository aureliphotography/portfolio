### ⏳ Need More Variety?
If you are looking for more than a few extra edits, extending the session is the most cost-effective way to grow your collection.

* More Retouched Images: Every additional 30 minutes adds 4 professional retouches to your package.
* Added Variety: Staying at the same prorated rate as your main shoot, an extension also provides a wider variety of pictures to select from.
